Apple Observation Agent

Local Liquid LFM2.5-VL compares the previous and current photo together with memory stored in RawTree, and only escalates to GPT-5 when the change justifies it.

Liquid (local) · RawTree memory · GPT-5 on demand
Input data

18-step apple sequence: healthy → early → advanced anthracnose

Real images collected with Nimble from the Mendeley apple disease dataset (9zgkwwv9j8). Simulated progression: distinct apples ordered by label and lesion score, one step per simulated day — not one physical apple over time. Labels are shown here only; they are never sent to the models.
